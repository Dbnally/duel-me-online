📜 Notice Board — How to Play & Colour Key

Walk with the joystick (bottom-left) or tap the ground. Tap a player (a near tap counts — no pixel-perfect needed) or use 👥 Players to challenge them. Pick a stake, they accept, you're both placed in the pit in view of the castle gate. Every second an exchange resolves: lock a style with the big round buttons (or keys 1/2/3: Melee/Magic/Range). First to 0 HP loses the pot. Leaving mid-duel = forfeit. Squire Pip (light-blue name) is a practice bot — 5/10/25 gp only.

🎨 THE COLOUR CODE (every colour also carries an icon or word)

🔺 THE TRIANGLE: 🟠 MELEE —hits→ 🟢 RANGE —hits→ 🔵 MAGIC —hits→ 🟠 MELEE
🗡️ MELEE = ORANGE — hits Range, big close hit, 6 dmg, “SLASH!”
✨ MAGIC = BLUE — hits Melee, spell bolt, 5 dmg, “ZAP!”
🏹 RANGE = GREEN — hits Magic, fast arrow shot, 4 dmg, “SNIPED!”
💥 Damage = RED hitsplat with white number
Result words wear the winning style’s colour: 🗡️ SLASH! orange · ✨ ZAP! blue · 🏹 SNIPED! green · CLASH! grey-white (same style both sides, both take 1)
❤️ HP is a single crimson bar — read health from its LENGTH and the number; it flashes only under 25%
⭕ Pit ring: WHITE/GREY stone free · GOLD pulsing challenge pending / countdown · RED duel live
🏆 WINNER + pot = GOLD · all money (gold pill, stake chips, pot, +GP floaters) is gold · lost stake floater is grey-red “-10 GP”
🗺️ Minimap dots: ⚪ white = you · 🟡 yellow = other players · 🔴 red = the two live fighters · 🔵 light blue = bot · 🪙 gold = shop stall
👤 Names: gold = you, and a challenger (with “!”) · red = your duel opponent · white = everyone else · light blue = Pip the bot
💬 Chat: white = players · gold = duel / system news · orange = challenge notices
